## Anti-examples (too narrow — wrong style)

These came from an earlier brainstorm. **Do not use** unless user explicitly wants procedural/historical content:

| Too narrow | Why it fails |
|------------|--------------|
| How Did Romans Get Clean Water? | Civilization-specific infrastructure |
| How Did Victorians Do Laundry? | Era procedure, no evolution hook |
| How Did Medieval Castles Get Built? | Craft/history documentary angle |
| How Did Tang China Run a Million-Person City? | Dynasty-specific governance |
| How Did People Preserve Food Before Refrigeration? | Cross-era procedure list |
| How Did Sailors Navigate Before GPS? | Tech history, weak personal hook |

### How to salvage a narrow idea

Reframe through the evolutionary lens:

| Narrow | Reframed question |
|--------|-------------------|
| How Did Romans Get Clean Water? | Why Did Clean Water Change Human Cities Forever? |
| How Did Victorians Do Laundry? | Why Would Your Ancestors Kill for Clean Clothes? |
| How Did People Preserve Food? | How Did Humans Accidentally Create Preserving Food? |

## Combination matrix (for fresh ideas)

Pick **one row** + **one column**, then phrase as a question:

| | Accidentally | Why weird | Reversal | Survival | Scarcity | Social |
|--|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Animals** | dogs, cooking fire | petting | cats/cows | hunting fear | honey | tribe roles |
| **Body** | laughter | babies | — | childbirth | pain hunger | grooming |
| **Mind** | language, music | dreams, boredom | — | sleep shifts | — | gossip |
| **Food** | agriculture | snacking | grain | famine | salt/sugar | sharing meals |

Example: Animals + Reversal → **Did Wolves Domesticate Humans Before Dogs Existed?**

## Script angle reminder

Once a topic is picked, the script should:

- Open with the question in the first 10 seconds
- Use "you" and "your ancestors" — not lecture tone
- Explain with **scenes** (hunt, camp, tribe, child, animal) — matches stick-figure pipeline
- Land a **modern punchline** (why your brain still does X)
- Stay under 5000 chars raw prose per `workflow/script-generation-prompt.md`
